
## Universal Data Model (UDM)
A technology-agnostic representation of business entities, attributes, and relationships, independent of storage, tooling, or implementation.

## Metadata-driven
An architectural approach where behavior, structure, and transformations are derived from declarative metadata rather than hard-coded logic.

## Ontology (AI context)
A machine-readable representation of domain concepts, relationships, constraints, and intent, suitable for reasoning by LLMs.

## Canonical data model
A single, business-aligned data structure that source systems are transformed into, so that every consumer downstream shares one definition of each concept instead of each source's own structure.
